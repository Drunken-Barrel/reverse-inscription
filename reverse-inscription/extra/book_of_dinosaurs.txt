Book of Dinosaurs

Page 1:
This guide book is made for dino-lovers and archaeologists alike! Every page contains 4 main pieces of information on each dinosaur listed, as can be seen labelled on the opposite page. Each puzzle requires only the information from the book provided alongside it, meaning that the bones in front of you are only from the dinosaurs listed ahead (but that does not necessarily mean that every dinosaur listed in the book is actually in the puzzle).
