Book of Dinosaurs



Every puzzle will have a book that contains pages 1-2, the pages of all dinosaurs present in the puzzle and sometimes red herrings as well. Pages will always be listed by their "real" number as defined by me (e.g. a puzzle may have pages 1, 2, 3, 5 and 8)



Page 1:

This guide book is made for dino-lovers and archaeologists alike! Each puzzle requires only the information from the book provided alongside it, meaning that the bones in front of you are only from the dinosaurs listed ahead (but that does not necessarily mean that every dinosaur listed in the book is actually in the puzzle).



Every page contains 5 main pieces of information on each dinosaur listed, as can be seen filled in with placeholders on the opposite page. We recommend paying the most attention to the "Information" section, as it has the most valuable, well, information for the puzzle.



Page 2 (example page):

Name: Somethingosaurus

Scientific name: *Something saurus*

Image: Gray silhouette of a dinosaur with the tail of an ankylosaur, body of a Spinosaurus and head of a *tyrannosaurus rex*, similar to a default pfp on Facebook

Description: *S.saurus*, more commonly known as somethingosaurus is an imaginary dinosaur made up as an example for this book.

Information: No real world accounts of *S.saurus* exist, but if they did this is the part where we'd tell you information like the minimum or maximum starting strength and health they could have, possible abilities and other traits unique to them.
